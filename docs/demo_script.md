# ImpactOS: 3-Minute Crisis Command Center Demo Script

**Scenario:** Severe Mutha River Flash Inundation & Trapped Citizen Rescue (Pune, Maharashtra)  
**Host / Presenter:** Incident Commander / Lead Systems Architect  
**Live UI:** `http://localhost:3000` | **API:** `http://localhost:8000`

---

### Minute 1:00 — From Chaos to Understanding (The Sensory Influx)

1. **Open the Landing Page / Command Center (`http://localhost:3000/landing`)**:
   - *"Welcome to ImpactOS. In any major disaster, the first 60 minutes are defined by pure chaos. Emergency dispatchers receive contradictory photos, fragmented social media chatter, and distress calls in regional dialects."*
   - Scroll through the 5 narrative milestones: **Chaos → Understanding → Verification → Action → Impact**.
   - Click **"Launch Command Center"** (`http://localhost:3000`).

2. **Demonstrate Raw Multimodal Ingestion (Tile 1)**:
   - Point out the **Bund Garden flood photo** showing turbulent brown water submerging riverside trees and the river wall.
   - Click Play on the **Marathi Voice Note** (*"माझी आजी अडकली आहे आणि ती बाहेर पडू शकत नाही"*).
   - Point to the real-time AI transcription:
     > *"Notice that the model did not just transcribe Marathi; it identified the core stakeholder: an elderly grandmother with mobility constraints trapped on a flooded ground floor."*

---

### Minute 2:00 — Truth Verification & Emergency Brutalism Mode

3. **Highlight the Trust Panel & Verification (Rule 1 & Rule 2)**:
   - *"Notice our non-negotiable rule: No fabricated numbers. The 94% Risk Score and 86% Consensus Confidence are computed from real multi-agent outputs."*
   - Expand the **Verified Evidence List**:
     - *Evidence 1:* Bund Garden visual water marks matched with *Parakh House* landmark signage.
     - *Evidence 2:* Verified distress audio acoustic profile.
     - *Evidence 3:* Mutha River hydrological gauge at `7.82m` (+1.32m over danger mark) with 35,400 cusecs Khadakwasla discharge (tagged `DEMO DATA`).
     - *Evidence 4:* Active IMD Station 43063 catchment warning.
   - Point to the **Mandatory Uncertainty Range**: `82% – 96%`.

4. **Demonstrate Emergency Mode & Manual Override**:
   - Show the high-contrast **Emergency Brutalism View**:
     - Large typography, crimson/black palette, 56px minimum tap targets.
     - Tap **"Navigate to Shelter (1.2 km)"** and show St. Helena Relief Shelter.
     - Click **"Switch to Command View (Override)"** to toggle back to the spatial bento grid.

5. **Explainability Replay ("Why?" Button)**:
   - Click **"Why?"** in the Consensus Panel.
   - Step through the 5-stage causal chain: Source → Intake → Intent → Verification → Risk → Decision.
   - Show the raw input payload and verified reasoning for each step.

---

### Minute 3:00 — Simulation, Optimization & One-Click Authorized Action

6. **Scenario Simulation & What-If Modeler**:
   - Scroll to the **Scenario Simulation View**:
     - Compare **"Act Now"** (25–45 min evacuation, minimal health risk) vs **"Delay 3 Hours"** (water rises 40cm, severe hypothermia risk).
     - Point out the mandatory stated assumptions under each scenario.

7. **Explainable Resource Optimization**:
   - Scroll to the **Resource Allocation Engine**:
     - Show why **NDRF Motorized Boat Team 4** (0.4 km away, ETA 4 min) was chosen over Team 7.
     - Show why **Sassoon ALS Ambulance 02** was assigned due to geriatric life-support equipment.

8. **Adaptive Multi-Persona Response**:
   - Switch between **Citizen** (safe route avoiding Ambedkar Bridge + bilingual Marathi/English alerts), **Volunteer** (muster coordinates + tasks), **Government** (legal statute + contingency budget), and **Media** (rumor-busting fact check).

9. **Human-in-the-Loop Action Execution (Rule 4)**:
   - Scroll to the Draft Actions section.
   - *"Nothing is transmitted externally without human authorization."*
   - Click **"Execute Approved Response"**:
     - Status updates to `APPROVED (LOGGED)`.
     - Manifest logs the operator signature and timestamp into the immutable audit trail.
   - Review the **Impact Dashboard** (145 people protected, response time cut to 11.4 mins) and **Crisis DNA** (99% match with August 2019 precedent).
